# FreeInBarna Mission

FreeInBarna helps people discover cultural activities and places that are free, without wasting time searching across disconnected sources. The product is designed to feel trustworthy, easy to browse, and useful for everyday planning.

## Purpose
The mission is to make free culture easier to discover and easier to trust. Users should be able to answer a few core questions quickly: what is free, where is it, when is it happening, and does it require an appointment or booking.

## Primary audience
- Local residents looking for free cultural activities nearby
- Visitors planning a day around free museums, exhibits, and community events
- People in Barcelona who want to find free activities and cultural places
- People using this project as a learning example for spec-driven development with AI coding agents
- People who want reliable information without checking multiple websites

## Product promise
FreeInBarna gives users a clean dashboard that surfaces free cultural options in an understandable way. It combines event information, museum free-day details, and scheduling notes so users can decide quickly and confidently.

## Stakeholder alignment
- Mary wants a reliable product built on a popular TypeScript stack with an easy-to-use dashboard.
- Susan needs museum free-day details, monthly free activities, and appointment requirements surfaced clearly.
- Steve wants an attractive, modern experience that works smoothly in contemporary browsers.

## Core principles
1. Trustworthiness: information should be clear and dependable.
2. Relevance: prioritize free cultural opportunities that matter locally.
3. Ease of access: dashboard layouts and filters should reduce friction.
4. Clarity: free days, schedules, and appointment rules should be explicit.
5. Scope focus: stay centered on free cultural discovery rather than broad commerce features.

## MVP scope
The initial release should focus on essentials:
- a curated list of free cultural activities and places
- museum information such as free days and schedules
- clear indicators for appointment requirements
- a dashboard that makes information easy to scan
- a polished browser experience that feels modern and dependable
